# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion the business outcome, secure organizational support, and make high-level decisions that are outside the delivery team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and the intended business outcomes
- Secure funding, staffing, or other organizational support
- Make major scope, priority, and go/no-go decisions
- Resolve escalations that the Project Manager cannot address within the team

### Collaboration
- Align with the Project Manager on progress, risks, and escalations
- Partner with the Product Manager on outcomes and major priority decisions
- Consult stakeholders when decisions affect their groups

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs and business workflows into clear, actionable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and impacts across processes
- Help refine backlog items and define measurable acceptance criteria

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager and stakeholders to clarify needs and priorities
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing to make requirements implementable and verifiable
- Keep the Project Manager informed about requirements dependencies and changes that affect delivery

---

## UX/Product Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/Product Designers and Researchers validate user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Consider usability and accessibility in design decisions

### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager on discovery, user needs, and product priorities
- Work with Developers to communicate designs and resolve implementation questions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility validation
- Share research findings with stakeholders and the Project Manager when they affect scope or plans

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and help the team deliver an interoperable, maintainable solution.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, integration, and technology decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and non-functional requirements
- Support technical estimates and surface implementation risks
- Promote maintainable design and appropriate engineering practices

### Collaboration
- Coordinate with Developers on design, implementation, and technical trade-offs
- Work with QA/Testing to make solutions testable and clarify technical quality expectations
- Keep the Project Manager informed about technical dependencies and risks
- Consult Security/Privacy Leads when design choices affect security or privacy

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security, privacy, and compliance needs throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify risks and recommend appropriate controls or mitigations
- Review relevant designs and release readiness evidence
- Escalate unresolved high-impact risks through the agreed incident or risk process

### Collaboration
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads/Architects to incorporate controls into the solution
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Partner with the Project Manager on risk tracking and escalation
- Align with the Release/Operations Owner on release risks and operational safeguards

---

## Release and Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Owners coordinate production readiness and the transition to ongoing service operation.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, release checks, and operational handoff
- Ensure monitoring, rollback, and support plans are prepared
- Track operational readiness and communicate post-release issues

### Collaboration
- Coordinate release timing and communications with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing on deployment checks and smoke tests
- Confirm security and operational risks with Security/Privacy Leads and Technical Leads
- Align with support teams and stakeholders on handoff and service-impacting updates

---

## Data and Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners ensure teams can measure whether delivered work achieves its intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define the instrumentation and reporting needed for agreed success metrics
- Establish measurement baselines and data-quality expectations
- Monitor results after release and communicate findings

### Collaboration
- Align with the Product Manager on success metrics and interpretation
- Work with Developers to implement and validate instrumentation
- Coordinate with the Release/Operations Owner on post-launch monitoring
- Share outcome reporting with the Project Manager and relevant stakeholders

---

## Applying Additional Roles
- Assign these roles when project scope, risk, or delivery needs require them; not every project needs a separate person for each role.
- Make ownership and decision authority explicit during planning. One person may hold multiple roles when appropriate, while keeping responsibilities and handoffs clear.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
